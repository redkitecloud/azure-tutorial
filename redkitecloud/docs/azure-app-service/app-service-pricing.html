Azure App Service Pricing

App Service pricing is straightforward — you pay for the App Service Plan, not for individual apps. Multiple apps on the same plan share its cost. Understanding the price points for each tier and how to optimise helps you run production apps affordably. This page covers all pricing tiers, real cost examples in Central India, and the top tips to reduce your bill.

How Billing Works

App Service billing is based on the App Service Plan — not individual apps:

  • You pay per hour for each instance in the plan
  • Multiple apps on the same plan = no extra app charge
  • The plan runs 24/7 unless you stop it (compute billing pauses when stopped)
  • Storage, custom domains, SSL certificates — included in plan cost
  • Outbound data transfer — charged separately (first 5 GB/month free)
ℹ️
Plan = The Meter Running Unlike serverless Functions (billed per execution), App Service Plans run continuously. A Basic B1 plan at ₹1,200/month charges that amount whether you have 1 request or 1 million. For very low traffic, Functions may be cheaper.

App Service Environment Pricing

ASE (Isolated tier) has two cost components:

  • ASE Stamp fee — Fixed hourly fee for the ASE infrastructure (~₹75,000–100,000/month)
  • Per-instance charge — Per Isolated plan instance (I1v2: ~₹10,000/month)

Total minimum ASE cost: ~₹85,000–110,000/month. Only justified for compliance, high security, or very large-scale single-tenant deployments.

Top 8 Cost-Saving Tips

1. Share Plans Across Multiple Apps

Multiple low-traffic apps (internal tools, staging) on one plan. No per-app charge.

2. Buy Reserved Instances for Production

1-year reservation saves ~33%. 3-year saves ~55%. Only for always-on production plans.

3. Stop Plans When Not Needed

Dev/test plans that aren't used nights/weekends — stop them. Compute billing pauses. Storage continues (~₹20/month).

4. Right-Size Before Reserving

Monitor CPU and memory for 2–4 weeks before committing. Reservations are for a specific VM size — can't change size without penalty.

5. Consider Functions for Very Low Traffic

If your app serves <1 million requests/month, Azure Functions Consumption plan may be cheaper (~₹0 for the first 1 million executions).

6. Use Scale-In Rules

Always define scale-in rules alongside scale-out rules. Without them, instances accumulate and costs grow.

7. Use Schedule-Based Scaling

Scale to minimum instances overnight and on weekends for predictable savings.

8. Monitor with Azure Cost Analysis

Check monthly App Service costs in Azure Cost Management. Look for orphaned plans (plans with no apps running), oversized instances, and unexpected scale-out events.
